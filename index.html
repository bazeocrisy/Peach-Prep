    Your Learning Path

    A suggested order through Grade 3 ELA. Work at your own pace; every lesson stays available.

    How the Learning Path works

    • Lessons come in a suggested order. It is not a test, and it does not place your child.
    • The path starts with D1 · Story elements. You can skip ahead or go back anytime.
    • Each lesson has a few short steps. One lesson can take more than one sitting.
    • Tick “Activities finished” when you are done, and the path moves on. The tick is a bookmark, not proof of mastery; check results stay separate.
    • You can open any lesson or switch modes anytime without clearing your progress.
