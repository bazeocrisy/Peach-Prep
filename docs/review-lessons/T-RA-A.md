## MUST
- [3.T.RA.1.a / passage p2 "Hunters of the Night", para 2, sentence 3] The sentence "The round, flat face of a barn owl helps send sounds to its ears." is a factual error. A barn owl's facial disk is heart-shaped, not round. Fix: "The flat, heart-shaped face of a barn owl helps send sounds to its ears."
- [SK-RA1a-P1] The clue gives the answer away. It ends "Which one asks about how bees do something?" The key is the only choice that mentions bees at all. The other three are about honey, jam and a kitchen, so the clue picks the key for the child. Fix: make the clue a strategy only, for example "Cross out questions about feelings and questions you already know the answer to. Which question is left that a book about bees could answer?" Also replace one distractor with an on-topic one that loses for a real reason, such as "Do honeybees live in hives?" (yes/no, and already known).
- [SK-RA2a-P2] The clue gives away Part B. "Choose the sentence that shows two fireflies answering each other" restates the key, "A female on the ground flashes back to answer him." It is the only choice with "answer" in it. Fix: "For Part B, choose the sentence that shows your Part A answer actually happening."

## SHOULD
- [SK-RA1a-P4] The key is the only choice about the dance. The clue says to "stay on the idea Rosa just learned," which leaves the child just one option. Fix: add a dance distractor the article already answers, for example "Do worker bees dance when they find flowers?", in place of "What color is a honeybee's body?"
- [SK-RA1a-T4] This item copies the shape of P3 almost exactly. It has two fact questions, one "Are X scarier/cooler than Y?" and one "Do I like/want…". The child can score from memory of the pattern. Fix: change the non-research rows, for example "Is it fun to watch owls?" and "Should my family get a pet owl?", and add a fact question that starts with "Where".
- [SK-RA1a-T5] The article already half-answers the key, "What can the bones in a pellet tell scientists?": paragraph 5 says pellets show "what owls eat." The `why` then rejects "Do most owls hunt at night?" because the article already says so, which undercuts its own logic. Fix: make the key "How do scientists find out which animal each bone came from?"
- [SK-RA1b-P2] The clue says "The fact must tell something about bees," but no choice mentions bees, the key included. A child who follows the clue finds nothing. Fix: change the clue to "Which fact explains why the bees' work matters to a pumpkin?" Or make the key "Bees carry pollen from one flower to another."
- [SK-RA1b-T1, T3, T4, T5] The test leans on one fact. "The moon has no air" or its space-suit consequence is in a T1 row, a T3 key, the T4 key and the T5 key. T4 asks the same thing as T1's "no air / Both" row. Fix: change T4 to a different both-sources fact, such as "The moon has dust and rocks on its ground" (Source 1 para 4, Source 2 para 3). Or change T5 to the footprints/no-wind link.
- [3.T.RA.1.c / Learn 1] Step 4 says to "join the ideas … with a linking word like also." The stated answer does not use "also" or any linking word; it joins the ideas with "and". Fix: change the answer to "Emperor penguins live in a very cold place where their feathers and fat keep them warm. They also huddle together to stay warm."
- [3.T.RA.1.c / Teach example] "Tiny green plants called algae" is not accurate: algae are not plants. Fix: "Tiny green living things called algae grow in a sloth's fur."
- [SK-RA1c-P2] The item is trivial. The prompt says "Leah's notes are all about milkweed," and the key is the only choice that contains the word milkweed. Fix: add a second milkweed distractor that does not fit Leah's notes, or that is not from Source 2. For example, "Milkweed juice tastes bad to birds" (Source 1, already in her notes).
- [SK-RA1c-T2] A distractor that the prompt presents as a "fact from Source 2" is not in Source 2. "They spend the summer in Georgia and the eastern United States." Source 2 never mentions the eastern United States. Fix: "They stay in Georgia all summer long."
- [SK-RA1c-T5] In slot 2, the key "In late summer" is the only time phrase among the options, and the clue says "ask what kind of word tells when." The slot tests nothing beyond spotting the time phrase, and it repeats the P4 move ("In the fall,"). Fix: add a second, wrong-time option, such as "In early spring", so the child has to check Source 2.
- [3.T.RA.2.b Practice vs Test] Every item uses the same three foils: an ad, a made-up story or cartoon, and a friend's or classmate's guess. T1 is P1 with the animal swapped. The test checks the pattern, not judgment. Fix: in at least one test item, use a subtler untrustworthy source, for example "a page where a person says they have seen sea turtles on vacation", or a trustworthy source that is only partly on topic.
