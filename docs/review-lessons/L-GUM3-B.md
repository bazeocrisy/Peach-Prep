All 80 Practice and Test items were solved blind before checking keys. Every key matched my answer except where noted below. All eight lessons match their `gum` row names and stay in grade-3 scope. Every Learn example's steps reach its stated answer.

## MUST
- [SK-GUM3-1-P4] "The frog's jumped into the pond." is not an error. It reads correctly as "The frog has jumped into the pond" (noun + 's for has), the same contraction the lesson's own Teach allows for it's ("short for it is or it has"). An adult can defend this sentence as correct, so the item has no defensible answer. Fix: make the plural unmistakable, e.g. "The two frog's jumped into the pond." or "Three frog's sat on a log." (avoid a verb that can follow has).
- [SK-GUM3-1-T1] Slot 2 option "Amaras'" is a correct possessive if the owner's name is Amaras. Nothing in the sentence says the name is Amara, so two options are defensible. Fix: options ["Amaras", "Amara", "Amara's"] (both distractors are wrong for any name), or name the owner earlier in the sentence.
- [SK-GUM3-1-T3] The clue gives away the answer. "Changes more than just dropping letters" rules out willn't, and "the apostrophe still goes where the o in not was" rules out wo'nt and wont, leaving only won't. Fix: clue such as "Say will not fast, the way you talk. Which word do you hear? Then check where the apostrophe goes."
- [SK-GUM3-8-T1] Two "wrong" splits can be defended by the lesson's only method (clap the beats). "mark- / et" and "ho- / ney" each break between two spoken beats; telling them from mar-ket and hon-ey needs the dictionary/phonics division rule (split between two consonants; after the consonant in a short-vowel syllable), which the lesson never teaches. Fix: teach the rule (see SHOULD below) or replace the distractors with splits that clapping clearly rejects, e.g. "ma- / rket" and "hone- / y".

## SHOULD
- [Lesson 8 Teach] "Clap the word" finds how many syllables but not where the boundary falls. P3 (wi-ndow), P4 (gard-en, mo-nkey), T2 (butt-erfly, bu-tterfly) and T4 (hu-ngry) all hinge on exact boundary placement. Fix: add a point such as "When two consonants come between vowels, split between them: bas-ket, win-dow, hun-gry. A dictionary shows where the syllables break."
- [Lesson 6 Teach] Teach says to use a reflexive pronoun "when the person doing the action is also the one it happens to," but keys and the parent note use other patterns: "by itself" (P1), "by myself" (T4), "The kids cleaned up the room themselves" (T4, emphatic), "I did it myself" (parent). The cleaning sentence does not fit the definition the child was taught. Fix: add a point: "You can also use a -self word after by (by myself means alone) or to show someone did it with no help: We did it ourselves."
- [Lesson 3 Teach point 5] "Time words like ... this morning tell you to use the past tense" is not always true ("I will run this morning"). Fix: replace "this morning" with "last night" or "two days ago".
- [SK-GUM3-4-T5, SK-GUM3-8-T1] mc items have 3 choices; SPEC §5 requires 4. Fix: add a fourth choice (e.g. "and" for 4-T5; a fourth split word for 8-T1).
- [SK-GUM3-7-P4] Key "Yusuf can sing and play the guitar." has no comma, yet the prompt asks for sentences that "use commas correctly." A careful child may reject a sentence with no comma. Fix: prompt "Choose the TWO sentences that are punctuated correctly."
- [SK-GUM3-4-P1, T1, T5] Clues name the exact job ("tells a result", "gives a choice") that Teach maps one-to-one to so/or, leaving only a recall step. Fix: keep the question part only ("Why did they bring umbrellas?", "Is the sentence asking you to pick?") and drop the job name.

## NIT

- [Lesson 2 Teach point 2] mouse to mice changes more than "the letters inside." Say "change their spelling."
- [SK-GUM3-5-T1 why] In "come home", home works as an adverb, and "her home can be seen" is not about this sentence. Drop home from the why or swap the choice for a clear concrete noun.
- [SK-GUM3-5-P2, T2] Clues "the group sing or the group sings" / "it fly or it flies" nearly hand over the answer. Consider "Is a choir one group or many things?"
- [SK-GUM3-7-P1] "We wanted to swim, but the pool was closed." nearly repeats the Lesson 4 Teach example ("I wanted to go swimming, but the pool was closed."). Use a fresh sentence.
- [SK-GUM3-2-T4] Test rows man, tooth, child, mouse all appeared in Learn or Practice. Swap one or two for goose, person or ox.
