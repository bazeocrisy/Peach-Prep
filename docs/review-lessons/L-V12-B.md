## MUST
- [SK-V1b-T5] The clue gives away the answer. "It is close to the name of the day" points straight to "elect" (Election Day), and none of the other choices (invite, thank, follow) looks anything like "Election." A child can match letters without knowing the word. Fix: change the clue to something like "Think of the social studies word for choosing a leader by voting. Try each word in the sentence: grown-ups vote to ___ a new mayor."
- [SK-V1b-T3] The `why` makes a false claim: "One exact word takes the place of four plain words." The phrase "come out of their eggs" has five words. Fix: "One exact word takes the place of five plain words," or "...takes the place of a whole phrase."

## SHOULD
- [3.L.V.1.b, whole lesson] This lesson largely duplicates 3.L.V.3.e ("Choosing the most exact word" in L-V3.js). Teach uses the same example (dog ate becomes gulped). Learn 1 trades "the wind was bad" for a stronger word, which matches V3e passage p2 sentence 4 ("the wind was really bad"). SK-V1b-P3 is the same said → whispered/shouted/asked item as V3e Learn 1. SK-V1b-P4 (good → delicious, with people-words as distractors) matches V3e Learn 2. The V1b standard ("Use grade-level vocabulary to enhance communication in a variety of settings") is about using the academic and content words a child is learning. Only P5 (pollen), T3 (hatch), T5 (elect) and Learn 3 (tadpoles) target that. A child who does both lessons sees the same item twice. Fix: refocus most Practice and Test items on swapping plain phrases for grade-level school words in science and social studies settings (for example: "the place where an animal lives" → habitat, "the people who live in a town" → citizens, "the leader of the town" → mayor). Replace P3 and P4 so they no longer copy V3e.
- [SK-V1b-P1, SK-V1b-P4] The distractors are too obviously wrong. In P1, all three wrong choices (kind, polite, friendly) describe people, so "ripe" is the only food word. In P4, helpful, brave, polite and early cannot describe a taste, so the two keys stand out. Fix: use near-miss distractors that could describe fruit or food but do not fit the details, for example P1 "rotten / tiny / green," and P4 "sour / burnt / cold" alongside the keys.
- [SK-V1a-P2] The clue nearly gives away the answer. "Find a sentence that starts with the word habitat" leaves only one target that qualifies (p1:2:2). Fix: drop the last sentence of the clue and keep the strategy: "Authors often explain a new word with the helper words is a or is the. Which sentence tells what a habitat is, not just that the marsh is one?"
- [SK-V2b-T3, Part B] Only two choices are plausible. "-ly" and "-er" do not appear in "brightness" at all, so the choice is really between "-ness" and "bright." Fix: use parts that come from the word or look like it, for example "-ness", "bright", "-ess", "-ight".
- [SK-V2b-P1 / 3.L.V.2.b Teach] Drummer is a confusing first example. Teach says -er turns an action word into a person word, and the clue asks "Is the base word an action or a describing word?" To an 8-year-old, "drum" is a thing, so the clue leads them to neither answer. The `why` has to explain that drum is a verb here. Fix: use an item whose base word is clearly an action (dancer, swimmer or singer, by adding a line to the passage), or rewrite the clue: "Cover -er. What does a person do with a drum? Then remember what -er does to an action word."
- [SK-V2b-P3] The test is too easy. The clue says to look for -er or -or endings, and the only choices that end that way are the two keys. The four distractors end in -ly, -ment and -ness. Fix: add a distractor that ends in -er but is not a person word, for example "louder" or "faster" (put one in the passage). This also practices the "Watch out" point in Teach.

## NIT
- [SK-V2a-P3] The `why` says "the base word is tied." The base word is "tie" (re + tie + d). Teach elsewhere tells children to check the base word, so this should be consistent. Fix: "Re- means again, and the base word is tie."
- [SK-V2c-P2] The `why` says "Repay means to pay back," but Teach says re- means again. Fix: "Repay means to pay again, or pay back."
- [SK-V1b-P2] The `why` claim "nobody paid at the tree" is not in the passage. At pick-your-own orchards, families do pay. Fix: "Bought does not fit because he took the peach right off the branch himself."
- [SK-V1b-T4] The `why` calls tiptoe "slow." Tiptoe really means quiet and careful. Fix: "Wander and stroll are slow ways to move, tiptoe is a quiet, careful way, and rest means to stop."
- [SK-V1b-T3] The test reuses "hatch," which is the key word in Learn 3 ("The eggs hatch into tadpoles"). Fix: use another content word for the test, or rewrite Learn 3 so it does not use hatch.
- [SK-V2b-T4] "Sailor" is the Teach example ("A sailor sails"). Fix: swap in another -or word, such as actor or editor.
- [3.L.V.1.a, p2 paragraph 5] "Some of the water soaks into the ground. The rest flows into rivers, lakes and oceans." This oversimplifies, because some rain falls straight into oceans or evaporates again. Fix: "Much of it flows into rivers, lakes and oceans."
- [3.L.V.1.a Teach words] The standard says "specialized" words, but the lesson calls them "content words." Fix: "content word (also called a specialized word)."
- [3.L.V.2.a Teach] The standard says "root words," but the lesson uses only "base word." Fix: add "(also called a root word)" to the base word definition, so a parent reading the standard sees the link.
