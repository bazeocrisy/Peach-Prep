- Wolves: howls carry for miles; a pack is a mother, father and young (National Geographic Kids).
- Water safety: swim near a lifeguard, life jackets for new swimmers, never swim alone, grown-up within arm's reach (American Red Cross, NC State). "Walk, don't run" was not confirmed and was left out.

- Warm-up/cool-down: warm-up raises heart and breathing rates, sends more blood and oxygen to warm muscles, 5-10 minutes; cool-down is 5-10 minutes of gentle movement, slows heart rate and breathing, helps avoid dizziness (KidsHealth). Injury-prevention and "drink water" claims were not confirmed and are not used.
