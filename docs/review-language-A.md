## L-GRM-03: Building strong sentences

All keys are correct and unique.

Two items look like they use Introduce or Continue skills. I checked both and they are fine:
- **-1, -2 and -3 (complex sentences and fragments).** These ask the child to *recognize* sentences built with *because* or *since*, which 3.L.GC.2.a assesses ("distinguish ... simple, compound, and complex"). The child is never asked to *use* a subordinating conjunction or an introductory comma. Keep it that way.
